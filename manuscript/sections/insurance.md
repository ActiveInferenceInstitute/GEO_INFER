## GEO-INFER-INSURANCE — Geospatial Underwriting and Claims Operations

GEO-INFER-INSURANCE provides underwriting, policy, claims, and pricing operations for geospatial insurance workflows within the GEO-INFER framework (module README). It belongs to the Governance, Risk & Domain theme, where risk transfer is modeled as a geospatial workflow: hazard exposure at a location drives underwriting decisions, pricing, and claim handling.

The `geo_infer_insurance` package exports insurance workflow components. Engines: `UnderwritingEngine`, `RiskAssessmentEngine`, `UnderwritingRulesEngine`, `UnderwritingDecisionEngine`, `PricingEngine`, `PolicyManager`, `ClaimsProcessor`, and `PortfolioManager` cover the full policy lifecycle from risk evaluation through portfolio oversight. Domain types: `Policy`, `Claim`, `UnderwritingCase`, and `Decision` give workflows a typed vocabulary. Convenience functions — `create_underwriting_engine`, `create_underwriting_system`, `underwrite_policy`, `underwrite_insurance_policy`, `process_claim`, `process_insurance_claim`, `assess_risk`, `calculate_premium` — provide one-call entry points for the common operations, with `create_underwriting_system(config)` building a defaults-or-configured stack directly in `__init__.py`.

The unit and integration tests exercise assessment, underwriting, pricing, and claims as typed workflow stages. Operation artifacts are inspectable outputs; regulatory compliance and actuarial calibration require separate evidence.
