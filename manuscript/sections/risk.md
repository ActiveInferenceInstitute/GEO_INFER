## GEO-INFER-RISK — Risk and Catastrophe Modeling

GEO-INFER-RISK supplies risk analysis and catastrophe modeling interfaces for insurance, hazard assessment, and resilience studies. Its package `geo_infer_risk` contains `core/`, `utils/`, `config/`, and a `civic_intel.py` integration surface, with module-level `config/` for scenario parameters.

The public interface, verified from `__init__.py`, splits into two families. General catastrophe modeling: `RiskModel`, `HazardModel`, `VulnerabilityModel`, `ExposureModel`, and `EnhancedExposureModel` compose the classic risk chain, extended by `EnhancedRiskEngine`, `MultiHazardInteractionMatrix`, and `calculate_compound_exceedance_probability` for interacting-hazard and compound-event analysis; constants `DEFAULT_CONFIDENCE_LEVEL` and `DEFAULT_RETURN_PERIODS` pin down deterministic defaults. Crescent City civic intelligence: `CRESCENT_CITY_GEO_INTEL_SCHEMA`, `CrescentCityAnchor`, `CrescentCityBounds`, `CrescentCityHazardIntel`, `MunicipalCodeSection`, `crescent_city_hazard_weights`, and loaders `load_crescent_city_hazard`/`parse_crescent_city_hazard` plus `create_risk_analysis` provide a schema-backed hazard-intelligence pipeline for a specific coastal community, with `CivicHazardDomain` typing the hazard categories.

The tests exercise model composition, compound-probability calculations, uncertainty bounds, and Crescent City schema round-trips. Calibrated risk predictions require domain data beyond these local fixtures.

Under the declared Module Themes, RISK belongs to Governance, risk and domain. It provides the consequence-modeling interfaces for combining hazard, exposure, and vulnerability under declared assumptions. Seeded local simulation supports repeatable computation; credible risk estimates additionally require justified input data, dependence assumptions, calibration, and uncertainty assessment. A fitted return-period quantile is expressed in the sampling period's units and does not establish an annual interpretation without a corresponding data model.
