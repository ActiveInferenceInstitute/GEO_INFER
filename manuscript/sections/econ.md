## GEO-INFER-ECON — Spatial Economic Modeling and Bioregional Market Design

GEO-INFER-ECON provides spatial economic modeling, market analysis, policy evaluation, and economic impact assessment with explicit geospatial dimensions (module README). It is the economic member of the Governance, Risk & Domain theme, complementing INSURANCE and HEALTH by quantifying monetary flows and policy interventions over place.

The package `geo_infer_econ` organizes its public surface into four groups, all re-exported from `src/geo_infer_econ/__init__.py`. The core engines — `EconomicModelingEngine`, `SpatialEconometricsEngine`, and `PolicyAnalysisEngine` — anchor general analysis. A microeconomics group supplies `ConsumerChoiceModels`, `ProducerTheoryModels`, `MarketStructureAnalysis`, `GameTheoryModels`, and `BehavioralEconomicsEngine`. A macroeconomics group covers `AggregateGrowthModels`, `BusinessCycleModels`, `MonetaryPolicyModels`, `FiscalPolicyModels`, and `TradeModels`. The bioregional group — `EcologicalEconomicsEngine`, `BioregionalMarketDesign`, `NaturalCapitalAccounting`, `EcosystemServicesValuation`, and `CircularEconomyModels` — is the module's distinguishing contribution, tying economic valuation to ecosystem structure. Supporting utilities (`EconomicAnalysisAPI`, `DataLoader`, `ResultsVisualizer`, `ModelValidator`, `EconomicIndicators`) expose API access, validation, and visualization. Configuration examples, microeconomics and bioregional guides under `docs/`, and runnable scripts under `examples/` (including `comprehensive_economic_analysis.py` and `integration_example.py`) document intended usage.

The unit and integration tests exercise the economic engines and their inputs. The tests and retained execution receipts provide scoped behavioral evidence rather than a claim of predictive skill.
