## GEO-INFER-LOG — Logistics and Supply-Chain Intelligence

GEO-INFER-LOG provides geospatial intelligence for logistics optimization, supply chain management, route optimization, and transportation planning (module README). It is an Infrastructure & Validation theme member by the repository's own theme table, though its character is operational: LOG is where the framework's spatial machinery meets moving vehicles and time-windowed deliveries.

The `geo_infer_log` package exports two distinct APIs from `src/geo_infer_log/__init__.py`. The observability API — `EnhancedLogger`, `PerformanceMetrics`, `LogAnalyzer`, `SpatialLogContext`, `get_logger` — is geospatially aware logging, instrumenting operations with location context. The logistics API is lazy-loaded from `core/` subpackages (via a module-level `__getattr__`, keeping import time low) and covers the operational stack: routing (`RouteOptimizer`, `LastMileRouter`, `MultiModalPlanner`, `VehicleRouter`, `TravelTimeEstimator`, `MultiObjectiveOptimizer`, with `Vehicle`, `VehicleType`, `RoutingParameters` as types), scheduling and tracking (`DeliveryScheduler`, `RealTimeTracker`, `FleetManager`), network and market analysis (`TransportationNetworkAnalyzer`, `ServiceAreaAnalyzer`), and sustainability plus simulation (`EmissionsCalculator`, `TrafficSimulator`, `SupplyChainModel`).

The unit and integration tests exercise logging and logistics operations. The logging and lazy logistics interfaces share a package, with their ownership and failure behavior checked separately.
