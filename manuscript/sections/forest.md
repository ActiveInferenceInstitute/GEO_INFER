## GEO-INFER-FOREST — Forest Management and Wildfire Risk

GEO-INFER-FOREST provides forest management, carbon sequestration, wildfire risk, and forest ecosystem analysis (module README). It is a Spatial & Place-based theme module, grounding the framework's ecological claims in one of the most geographically explicit domains: standing biomass, canopy structure, and fire danger are all functions of place and time.

The `geo_infer_forest` package exports public symbols from `src/geo_infer_forest/__init__.py`, spanning four concerns. Inventory and health: `ForestInventory` and `ForestHealthMonitor` track stock and condition, with `CanopyAnalyzer` for structural analysis and `DeforestationDetector` for change detection. Carbon: `CarbonSequestrationModeler` quantifies uptake and storage. Wildfire: `WildfireRiskAnalyzer` and `FireRiskAssessor` assess hazard, supported by the data types `FireIncident`, `FireWeatherObservation`, and the enumerations `FuelType` and `FireDangerRating`. This split mirrors how forestry science itself partitions the problem — state assessment, carbon accounting, and disturbance risk are separate modeling lines over shared geographic data.

The unit and integration tests exercise inventory, health, carbon, and wildfire analysis. Enumerations such as `FuelType` and `FireDangerRating` make domain vocabulary explicit; accuracy against field observations requires separate evidence.
